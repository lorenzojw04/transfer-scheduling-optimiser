# transfer-scheduling-optimiser

Welcome to my Missionary Transfer Scheduler project

I lived in Italy as a Missionary for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and managed end-to-end travel logistics for approximately 160 missionaries across Northern Italy and Switzerland within the Italy, Milan Mission. Responsibilities included planning and booking all multi-modal transportation, such as trains, flights, car rentals, taxis, and hotels, for complex transfer cycles every six weeks. 

To ensure safety and logistical support with heavy luggage and limited connectivity, travel schedules were structured using a specialized manual routing algorithm known as the "snake method," creating dependent sequences that paired transferring missionaries together. 

All timetables, routing connections, and ticket purchases were meticulously coordinated by hand to guarantee feasible and efficient transit.

**This project is here to make everything easier.**
